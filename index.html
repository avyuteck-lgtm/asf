Serving humanity with compassion, dignity and transparency.

We run free health check-up and eye screening camps, give schoolchildren books, stationery and tiffin boxes, and feed Hyderabad's stray dogs. We're a new foundation with a five-year plan, and we report openly on how every donation is used.

Newly founded, already at work

We started with what people around us needed most. These programmes are running today, ahead of the larger plan for 2026 to 2031.

  • Free medical health check-up campsDoctor consultations, BP, sugar and oxygen checks, and health counselling.
  • Free eye screening campsVision tests and cataract checks by ophthalmologists and optometrists.
  • Hospital referral supportPatients who need further care are referred to partner hospitals.
  • Books and stationeryNotebooks, pencils and books for underprivileged students.
  • Tiffin boxes for studentsGiven to underprivileged students to support nutrition at school.
  • Feeding stray dogsRegular feeding, and awareness about compassion for animals.
  • Community volunteer activitiesNeighbours, students and professionals giving time to local causes.

What happens at a free camp

We hold camps in villages, schools, orphanages, old-age homes and low-income urban areas. Each one focuses on early detection, advice and referral.

Health check-up

  • General physician consultation
  • Blood pressure and blood sugar tests
  • BMI, temperature, pulse and oxygen checks
  • Health counselling
  • Medicines, subject to availability
  • Referral to partner hospitals

Eye screening

  • Vision testing
  • Eye pressure screening
  • Cataract identification
  • Refractive error detection
  • Diabetic eye awareness
  • Eye care counselling

Who we reach

  • Senior citizens
  • Women and children
  • Daily wage workers
  • Urban poor and rural communities
  • Persons with disabilities

From Hyderabad outward

We grow one phase at a time, as volunteers, partners and funding allow.

  1. Phase I

    Hyderabad

    Health camps, education support, volunteer mobilisation and hospital partnerships.

  2. Phase II

    Telangana

    District-level work through local volunteers and partner institutions.

  3. Phase III

    South India

    Neighbouring states, through strategic partnerships.

  4. Phase IV

    Pan-India

    Major cities and selected rural districts, through regional chapters.
